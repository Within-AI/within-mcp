The motion has three phases. [Discover](/transformation/company-brain) captures how work happens, [Structure](/transformation/company-brain#structure) makes sense of it, and Improve builds skills and agents on top. Within runs this as a continuous loop rather than a one-time project. See [Discover → Structure → Improve](/concepts/discover-structure-improve) for the model itself; these pages are how you run it.

Start by naming a high-level business objective and the technology stack you want to build within. Within respects the enterprise stack and won't recommend a competitor's tools.

## The Company Brain

These pages use **Company Brain** for the living map of how your organization actually runs. It is what the portal's two intelligence layers add up to: the [Process Index](/concepts/process-index), which holds every process with its steps, dependencies, and versions, and [Artifacts](/concepts/artifacts), the source documents and recordings behind them.

Everything you build draws on it. That is what separates a build grounded in how your business actually works from one built against a generic template.
