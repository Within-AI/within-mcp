# Evaluate work hypotheses and choose useful assistance

## Three independent questions

1. **Can a model help with the transformation?** Variable language, context or evidence may require interpretation, synthesis, generation or feedback-driven problem solving. Identify inputs, intent, standard and output. Complexity from unclear goals, broken permissions or stakeholder conflict is not evidence of model fit.
2. **Can the work appropriately be delegated?** Preserve personal intent, relationships, accountable judgment and required approvals. Useful evidence preparation can be separated from the final consequential decision.
3. **Would the help create net value?** Compare accepted quality and all human effort against current practice and the best simpler alternative, at supported demand.

Repetition and reuse strengthen an economic case only after useful fit is established. Many users are not necessary: one specialist's high-value job can qualify. Frequent fixed transfers may need ordinary software. An infrequent ambiguous strategic decision may need human ownership with bounded preparation help. Do not classify whole professions: factual email drafts can fit supplied author intent; coding can fail without a usable specification; design exploration can fit a brief while product direction remains human.

## Decision order

| Establish | Ask | Consequence if unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Useful model contribution | What costly semantic work or adaptive investigation could improve? | Prefer process change or conventional software when no model is needed. |
| Intent and boundary | Who wants the output, what counts as useful, where does the job stop? | Investigate or narrow before specifying a solution. |
| Context and standard | Is the correct entity/version, current expertise and acceptance standard available? Who owns it? | Request decisive sources; more repetition does not repair missing context. |
| Checkability and containment | Can serious errors and omissions be caught before harm? Is checking cheaper than doing? | Test on independent cases or narrow scope; citations/tests alone do not prove completeness. |
| People's purpose | Which personal decisions, relationships or key controls must remain? | Keep the steps and responsibilities a named person holds in the future state. |
| Net value | Does useful effort avoided or quality gained exceed preparation, review, failures and upkeep? | Keep impact provisional; avoid unsupported hours or ROI. |
| Implementation readiness | Are required platforms, exact interfaces, access, control and ownership available? | State the dependency; preserve the underlying work hypothesis if useful. |

Keep confidence separate for work shape, recurrence, variation/tail coverage, output quality, access and value. Give each a reason, source and missing evidence. Many duplicate observations do not raise confidence in different cases. Thin capture lowers confidence in observed recurrence and variation; it is not proof that the transformation is unsuitable.

## Assumptions, counter-hypotheses and “ignore if”

For each candidate, write:

- **Claim:** the work and expected useful change.
- **Necessary assumptions:** what must be true, sourced or explicitly unknown.
- **Strongest credible competing explanation:** why the same observed pain might have another cause and the proposed help might add work or miss the bottleneck.
- **Discriminating evidence/test:** the smallest comparison that separates the explanations.
- **Ignore / change / stop if:** a concrete condition that would reject or redirect the recommendation.

Example: after customer calls a seller reviews evidence and updates qualification fields. A source-linked draft might save interpretation effort. But the dominant delay may be failed CRM saves or missing call capture, so another drafting agent would add review without fixing the bottleneck. Request distinct input/accepted-output pairs and extraction/review/save effort or error evidence. Compare repair versus drafting on the same eligible cases. If saving dominates, repair first; if checking takes as much effort at equal quality, narrow or reject drafting. This is an example, not a fact about any uninspected workspace.

Generic “accuracy risk” is not a counter-hypothesis. Invite a correction such as “those processes do not connect,” “we already have this,” or “that is not where the time goes.” Keep it visible near value, not buried in a scorecard.

## Forms to compare

Consider only plausible, permitted alternatives. Do not force a fixed number.

| Form | Reason to consider it |
| --- | --- |
| No change / process change | Remove unnecessary or duplicated work; improvement may not justify upkeep. |
| Reuse / repair / extend existing assistance | Existing help may meet the need or fail at one fixable boundary. |
| Native feature / ordinary automation | Stable transfers, arithmetic, routing, validation or already-owned capability. |
| One model call / reusable skill | Bounded transformation with supplied inputs and an inspectable standard. |
| Retrieval-backed / interactive assistance | Changing questions reuse maintained expertise while a human provides intent. |
| Triggered workflow with model steps | Fixed sequence/destination, with bounded semantic transformation. |
| Adaptive agent | Tool choice, investigation and revision depend on intermediate evidence, with stopping checks and recovery. |

Model usefulness alone does not justify an adaptive agent. State why the additional state, tools or autonomy beat a simpler useful form. Do not assume a particular platform from an example sentence; respect the intake and verify current product capabilities in research.

## Proposed future state and readiness

Account for removed, combined, retained and added work, including context preparation, verification, correction and exceptions. Preserve the purpose of navigation/context steps even when a tool replaces the clicks. Preserve each named key control and the accountable reviewer; moving a step cannot erase its authority requirement.

Map inputs and outputs to correct business objects/versions. Describe triggers, stop/no-answer behavior, duplicate prevention, recovery, source freshness, standard owner and maintenance. Propose write behavior only within allowed constraints and with actual interface evidence; documented API capability is not a tested grant. Unknowns do not become “neutral approval.”

For discovery, unknowns become information requests. Before a solution is called [ready to design](../SKILL.md#stage-2--investigate-the-selected-hypothesis) (the Stage 2 verdict that replaces calling a trial ready), resolve its required intent, essential context, permissions, accepted-result standard, critical error checks and owner; the success judgement and owner go in the design brief's objective and the critical errors in its important errors. Before build readiness, also resolve required controls, deployment access, integration and ongoing ownership. Judge at the intended boundary: manual file input or human-applied drafts can avoid a write-access dependency. A prohibited form may be rejected while another remains viable.

## Impact and validation

Use qualitative High/medium/low relative to the stated objective, with reasoning; say “Not assessed” where no defensible basis exists. Separate expected impact, evidence confidence and readiness. Explain who benefits, supported cadence/reuse, total effort and challenges. Avoid multiplying raw records by guessed minutes or counting shared upstream preparation in multiple solutions.

Net benefit is a testable argument: useful effort avoided + quality gain − preparation − supervision − review/correction − failures − maintenance. Compare current practice, the simpler alternative and the proposed help on equivalent eligible cases. Before a trial agree on acceptance, serious errors and accountable approval; measure total human effort, accepted output quality, exceptions, maintained use and upkeep. More generated artifacts alone is not success.

## Existing criteria

The unchanged 33-criterion vocabulary is retained in [legacy/config/criteria.json](../legacy/config/criteria.json). Read relevant definitions during selected investigation; do not run its tier scoring by default. Provenance remains `captured`, `derived`, `researched`, `supplied` or `assumed`, with a precise source or explanation for each judgment. An assumption is not evidence.

Retain particular attention to Output Checkability, Detectability, Task Boundedness, Human Execution Value, Customer Policy, Read/Write Access, Value Profile, Annual Volume, Active Time and variation. Ask the owner for facts only they can establish. Identify the precise source when overriding generated process attributes.

The old weights, capture bands, draft bonus, highest-tier ceiling, and Annual Volume 501+ / Active Time below 15 minutes interplays are historical framework assumptions, not empirically established universal boundaries. They remain unchanged in the optional legacy engine for reproducible comparisons; they do not choose v4 recommendations. Do not replace them with new arbitrary weights. If quantitative ranking is explicitly requested, disclose its assumptions, missing data and sensitivity; no score can override a prohibited action or missing foundation at the readiness boundary.
