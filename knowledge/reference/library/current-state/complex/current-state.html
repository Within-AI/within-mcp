How to read this

  • Bands show who does the work. Left to right is the order of the work; systems are rails at the bottom that steps read from and write to.
  • Bank enrichment and remittance posting only; downstream MOA context is adjacent and is not a Bank workflow step. Upstream bot / UiPath Action Center intake is adjacent and is not a Bank workflow step.
  • interaction_surfaces on each interaction, reconciliation, and transfer node enumerate the kinds of systems the step touches with their integration class and a controlled role from {read, write, source, target, unknown}. Role 'source'/'target' mark the two ends of a write/transfer seam; role 'unknown' is used where the frozen source does not establish the transfer mode (e.g. a drafted outbound email whose send is left unsent / out of scope). Surfaces carry no trigger, precedence, or consultation-condition detail; that hidden logic belongs to Layer 2. The lists are retained deliberately so the active system reads and writes each step requires remain visible for interface design.
